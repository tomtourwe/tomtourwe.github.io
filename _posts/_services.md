title: "Services I offer as fractional Head of AI"
layout: post
---

I offer support as a fractional head of AI for companies working on AI-driven innovation projects, often in the context of VLAIO funding.

In this role, I take temporary responsibility for the AI framing and positioning of the project and for coordinating with external partners and VLAIO. The engagement is modular: depending on your needs, I can take on a single, well-defined task or support multiple steps across the process.

All activities below fall under this fractional head of AI role, but none are mandatory.




---

### Assessing the AI Idea and VLAIO Relevance

I assess the AI idea and its relevance across VLAIO funding programs.

This includes:
* evaluating feasibility and maturity,
* identifying where the AI work constitutes innovation,
* mapping the project to relevant VLAIO instruments,
* identifying eligibility constraints and major risks.

The outcome is a clear recommendation on whether and where to apply.

---

### Selecting an External Proposal Writing Partner

I support the selection of an external proposal writing partner.

This includes:
* identifying potential proposal writers,
* contacting them and discussing what is and is not included in their services,
* assessing their experience with AI-driven innovation projects,
* helping select one or two suitable candidates.

I do not write the proposal text myself. My role is to act as an expert counterpart to the writer, ensuring that the AI-related content is credible, consistent, and aligned with the project.

---

### Selecting an AI Partner

If the project requires an external AI partner, I support the selection process.

This includes:
* identifying potential AI partners,
* discussing scope, responsibilities, and expectations,
* assessing their expertise and suitability.

---

### Acting as AI Point of Contact

I act as the AI point of contact throughout the preparation phase.

This includes:
* representing the AI aspects of the project in discussions with partners,
* coordinating between proposal writers, AI partners, and internal teams,
* interacting with VLAIO on AI-related questions when needed.

This provides senior AI oversight without a full-time hire.

---

### Review Before Submission

Before submission, I review the final proposal draft based on experience with AI projects and innovation funding.

This includes:
* assessing feasibility and credibility,
* checking internal consistency of the AI narrative,
* evaluating alignment with the chosen funding instrument.

This is a review step, not a rewrite.

---

### After a Successful Proposal

Support does not need to stop after a successful proposal.

Depending on the project and your needs, I can:
* act as an AI partner during execution,
* coordinate the AI workstream for larger projects,
* support the hiring and onboarding of an internal AI team,
* remain involved as fractional head of AI during project execution.

This allows continuity between proposal, funding, and implementation.
