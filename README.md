I'm a software and AI engineer with a PhD in Computer Science and 15+ years of experience in AI-related projects. My background is in applied AI and software engineering research for large-scale industrial software systems.

- After my PhD, I've worked with [ASML](https://www.asml.com/) on advanced code analysis algorithms for migrating their software toward aspect-oriented modularization.
- I was the Tech Lead of the Data and Artificial Intelligence Competence Lab at [Sirris](https://www.sirris.be), the research center for the Belgian industry, where I led industrial R&D projects applying AI to predictive modeling, profiling, production optimization, and product usage monitoring.
- I worked at [Oqton](https://oqton.com/) as Head of AI, leading work on deep learning for 2D and 3D computer vision in the additive manufacturing industry.
- I'm currently at [CodeLaser.io](https://codelaser.io/), working as a Principal Software Engineer on large-scale LLM-based software modernization

Over the years, I've gathered extensive experience writing, executing, and managing R&D projects at both national and EU level. I'm also regularly serving as an expert reviewer for [VLAIO](https://www.vlaio.be/), the organisation that provides funding for innovation & entrepreneurship in Flanders, Belgium. 

On this website, I share practical insights and lessons learned from years of working at the intersection of AI, software engineering, and applied research, with a particular focus on what makes a strong R&D proposal—and common pitfalls to avoid.
